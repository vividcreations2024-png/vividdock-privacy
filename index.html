VividDock Privacy Policy

Developed by Vivid Creations Technologies

Effective Date: November 21, 2026  |  Last Updated: November 21, 2026

VividDock is developed by Vivid Creations Technologies. This Privacy Policy explains how VividDock handles information when you use the application.

Contact: vividcreations2024@gmail.com

1. Overview

VividDock is a floating dock and productivity utility application.

VividDock does not require you to create an account and does not provide a VividDock cloud account or cloud synchronization service.

Where practical, information created by you is stored locally on your device.

VividDock may use third-party services, including Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), which may process certain information according to their own privacy policies and applicable requirements.

2. Information Stored Locally

VividDock can store application data locally on your device, including:

  • App settings and preferences
  • Dock configuration
  • Notes
  • Tasks and checklists
  • Speed Dial entries
  • Clipboard history, if you enable the clipboard feature
  • Other information required for the application's features

This information is stored within the application's local storage and is not uploaded to a VividDock server.

3. Clipboard Data

If you enable clipboard history, VividDock may access clipboard content so that previously copied items can be displayed in the clipboard history feature.

Clipboard history:

  • Is stored locally on your device.
  • Is not uploaded to VividDock servers.
  • Is not intentionally used for advertising or advertising profiling.
  • Can be deleted through the application.
  • Is automatically removed when the relevant local application data is cleared or the application is uninstalled.

Clipboard text payloads stored by VividDock are encrypted using AES-256-GCM with keys managed through the Android KeyStore.

Other application data stored in the local database is not represented as being fully database-encrypted.

4. Location Information

VividDock may request location permission when you use the weather feature.

Location information is used to determine weather information for your current location through the Open-Meteo weather service.

VividDock:

  • Does not continuously track your location in the background for the weather feature.
  • Does not maintain a location history or location trail.
  • Does not use location for advertising profiling.

The weather feature uses Android's location services and may obtain location information from available device location providers.

Weather requests are made to the Open-Meteo service. VividDock does not require an API key or user account for these requests.

5. Camera and QR/Barcode Scanning

VividDock may use the device camera for QR code and barcode scanning.

Camera frames are processed on the device using Android CameraX and Google ML Kit.

VividDock does not intentionally upload or permanently store camera images or video for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

VividDock may also use the camera hardware's torch/flashlight functionality when you request it.

6. Screen Capture

Certain VividDock features, such as the screen color picker and screenshot functionality, may use Android's MediaProjection screen-capture framework.

Screen capture is initiated only after the user requests the relevant feature and grants the Android system's screen-capture permission.

VividDock does not use MediaProjection for continuous background screen monitoring.

Captured screen information is used for the requested feature and is not intentionally collected for advertising or user profiling.

Screen capture functionality is separate from VividDock's AccessibilityService functionality.

7. AccessibilityService

VividDock provides optional Android AccessibilityService functionality for specific utility features.

After you request to enable the feature, VividDock displays an in-app disclosure explaining why Accessibility access is required. You must affirmatively continue to Android Settings before the service can be enabled.

The AccessibilityService is used for features such as:

  • Global navigation shortcuts.
  • Detecting the current foreground application for per-app dock auto-hide behavior.

VividDock's AccessibilityService is configured without access to window content.

VividDock does not intentionally use AccessibilityService to:

  • Read passwords.
  • Read keystrokes.
  • Read credit-card information.
  • Read private messages.
  • Read email contents.
  • Capture screen text or view hierarchies.
  • Collect unrelated personal information.

Accessibility access is optional and can be disabled through Android system settings.

8. Phone Calls and Speed Dial

If you use the Speed Dial calling feature, VividDock may request the Android CALL_PHONE permission.

The permission is requested when you explicitly initiate a call from a Speed Dial entry.

If the permission is not granted, VividDock can fall back to opening the Android dialer so that you can place the call yourself.

Speed Dial names and phone numbers are stored locally on your device.

VividDock does not record phone calls or collect call audio.

9. Media Controls

VividDock may use Android media-session functionality to provide media playback controls.

This allows VividDock to interact with active media sessions for functions such as:

  • Play/pause
  • Previous/next track
  • Other supported media controls

VividDock's media functionality is not designed to collect or inspect the contents of private messages, emails, or unrelated notifications.

10. Advertising

VividDock may display advertisements using Google AdMob.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • Device identifiers
  • IP address
  • Device and operating-system information
  • Approximate location
  • Advertising interactions
  • Diagnostics and related technical information

The exact information processed can depend on your device, region, consent choices, and applicable advertising configuration.

VividDock does not sell your personal information.

11. Consent and Privacy Choices

VividDock uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable consent requirements for advertising.

Depending on your location and applicable regulations, you may be presented with choices regarding personalized advertising and related data processing.

Where supported, privacy choices can be reviewed or changed through the privacy options provided by VividDock.

Your available choices may vary depending on your location and applicable laws.

12. Data Sharing

VividDock does not sell your personal information.

VividDock does not operate its own cloud database for storing your VividDock notes, tasks, clipboard history, Speed Dial entries, or dock configuration.

Certain third-party services may process information when their functionality is used, including:

  • Google AdMob — advertising and related diagnostics.
  • Google UMP — consent management.
  • Open-Meteo — weather information requests when the weather feature is used.
  • Google ML Kit — on-device barcode and QR-code processing.

These services operate under their respective terms and privacy policies.

13. Data Security

VividDock takes reasonable measures to protect locally stored application information.

Clipboard text payloads stored by VividDock are encrypted using AES-256-GCM with encryption keys protected by the Android KeyStore.

VividDock does not claim that all application data or the entire local Room database is encrypted.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

14. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored VividDock information generally remains on your device until you:

  • Delete it through the application where a deletion option is provided.
  • Clear VividDock's application data.
  • Uninstall VividDock.

VividDock does not maintain a separate VividDock cloud account containing this local application data.

Third-party services may retain information according to their own policies and applicable requirements.

15. Children's Privacy

VividDock is not directed specifically toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a VividDock account or VividDock-operated server.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when VividDock's features, data practices, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements change.

The Last Updated date at the top of this policy indicates when it was most recently revised.
